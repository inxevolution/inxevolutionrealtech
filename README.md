# In X Evolution

> **Strategic and enterprise baseline — Draft 0.4 — 23 November 2026**

In X Evolution is proposed as a brand-led digital business ecosystem that creates, integrates, curates and operates useful digital products and services across technology, digital commerce, investment-related information and online communities.

This is a strategic proposal for owner approval. It does not establish a registered corporate group, live products, a particular legal entity or permission to provide regulated financial services.

## Purpose

Create useful, trustworthy and commercially sustainable digital products and services that help people and organisations use technology, access relevant digital tools and information, and participate in well-governed digital communities and commerce.

## Mission

Design, integrate, curate and continuously improve accessible digital solutions and services. Operate transparently, protect user data, explain third-party relationships and build long-term value through responsible execution.

## Vision

Build a recognised, trusted and modular digital ecosystem in which technology, commerce, information and community reinforce one another without compromising user trust, security or regulatory responsibilities.

## Century-scale ambition

Design In X Evolution to remain adaptable, governable, useful and capable of transferring knowledge and responsibility across generations. The 100-year horizon is a stewardship framework, not a prediction or guarantee. Products, business models, legal structures, vendors and technologies must remain replaceable.

See [100-Year Longevity Strategy](docs/LONGEVITY_STRATEGY.md) and the [Wiki longevity page](docs/wiki/100-Year-Longevity-Plan.md).

## Strategic portfolio — proposed

| Area | Proposed role |
|---|---|
| Central In X Evolution | Strategy, governance, brand, finance oversight and shared services |
| Real Technology | Digital products, software, automation, integration and technical services |
| Invest | Investment-related information, research/education and internal investment/mining operations; regulated scope requires review |
| SHOP | Digital catalogue, own/third-party commerce and disclosed partner offers |
| Real Telegram / Community | Communication, education, moderated community and support |

Portfolio names and public references require owner confirmation. They do not establish legal ownership, service availability or regulatory permissions.

## Working principles

1. Evidence before assumptions.
2. Customer value before feature volume.
3. Requirements before technology selection.
4. Security, privacy and legal review by design.
5. Clear separation between internal operations, customer services and regulated activities.
6. Small, reviewable and reversible changes.
7. Transparent pricing, risks and commercial relationships.
8. Traceability from objective to requirement, implementation, test and release.
9. Preserve critical knowledge while keeping technologies and business models replaceable.

## Scope

This repository covers In X Evolution only. Separate projects must not be added by assumption. Each business line requires an owner, scope, budget, risk assessment and approval before launch.
