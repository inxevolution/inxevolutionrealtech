01 / Purpose

Useful innovation.
Built responsibly.

Our proposed purpose is to design, integrate and continuously improve digital products and services that help people and organisations use technology, access relevant information and participate in transparent digital commerce and communities.

Project status

In X Evolution is currently in strategy, discovery and technical planning. This page presents the proposed direction; it does not claim that the listed business lines, products or services are already operational.

02 / Proposed portfolio

One ecosystem.
Distinct capabilities.

Shared governance and standards, with clear responsibilities and risk boundaries for each activity.

Real Technology

Digital products, software development, automation, integration and technical services.

02 — INFORMATION & RESEARCH

Investment-related information, research and education, plus internal operational activities. Regulated services require separate legal assessment and authorisation where applicable.

Enterprise Operations

03 / How we intend to work

Trust is an operating principle.

Long-term value depends on disciplined execution, clear accountability and the willingness to improve.

Integrity

Be transparent about capabilities, limitations and relationships.

Security & privacy

Protect information and apply access controls by design.

Continuous improvement

Use evidence, testing and feedback to guide change.

Resilience

Keep knowledge, systems and business models adaptable.

04 / Long-term perspective

Designed for change.

The 100-year horizon is a stewardship ambition: preserve the ability to adapt and create value, not preserve today's products or technology unchanged.

2026–2027

Foundation

Confirm direction, ownership, first pilot, requirements and technical foundations.

2027–2031

Validation

Test real customer needs, validate offers and establish sustainable operating practices.

2031 onward

Adaptation

Expand only where evidence supports it; renew capabilities, governance and technology over time.
