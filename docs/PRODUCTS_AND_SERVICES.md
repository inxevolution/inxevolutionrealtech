## 1. Portfolio policy

The portfolio below translates the current In X Evolution direction into offer hypotheses. A name or link appearing in public material does not mean that a product is built, owned, available, priced or legally approved.

Each offer must pass a launch gate: customer problem, product owner, scope, delivery capability, price and cost model, terms, privacy/security review, support and legal review.

## 4. Customer value propositions — hypotheses

- **Technology customers:** practical digital solutions with clear scope, transparent delivery and support.
- **Invest information users:** understandable, sourced and risk-aware information about digital finance tools and platforms.
- **SHOP customers:** clearly described products and transparent purchasing terms.
- **Community members:** useful information, access to updates and responsible moderation.
- **Partners:** clear integration terms, referral attribution and accountable relationship management.

Validate these propositions with real users before making them public promises.

## 5. Product lifecycle

1. Idea logged with owner and target user.
2. Problem and demand validated.
3. Legal, privacy, security and dependency risks reviewed.
4. Business case and unit economics prepared.
5. MVP scope and acceptance criteria approved.
6. Build/buy/partner decision recorded.
7. Pilot and user acceptance completed.
8. Launch approval and operational readiness.
9. Performance, support and risk monitored.
10. Continue, improve, pause or retire based on evidence.

## 6. Minimum product record

Every product or service should have:
- Unique ID and name.
- Business line and accountable owner.
- Product/service description and target user.
- Problem statement and value proposition.
- Delivery model and dependencies.
- Seller/provider and commercial relationship.
- Price, currency, cost and revenue model.
- Contract, licence, refund and support terms.
- Data processed and security classification.
- Legal/regulatory review status.
- Launch status, version and lifecycle dates.
- KPIs, feedback and known limitations.

## 7. Explicit non-promises

Until validated and authorised, In X Evolution must not promise:
- Investment returns, mining yields or token appreciation.
- Risk-free or guaranteed financial results.
- Ownership or endorsement of third-party platforms without evidence.
- Unverified certifications, licences or regulatory status.
- Technical availability, support levels or delivery dates that have not been agreed.
