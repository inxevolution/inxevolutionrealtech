# In X Evolution — Business Strategy

**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 23 November 2026  
**Status:** Proposed strategic definition — owner approval required

## 1. Executive definition

**In X Evolution is a brand-led digital business ecosystem that creates, integrates, curates and operates useful digital products and services across technology, digital commerce, investment-related information and online communities.**

The ecosystem should use shared governance, brand standards, customer processes, data controls and performance reporting while keeping each business line's risks, economics and responsibilities visible.

This is a strategic proposal for the project. It is not evidence of a registered corporate group, a legally incorporated holding company, or an already operational multi-company structure.

## 2. Purpose

Create practical value by helping people and organisations use technology, access relevant digital tools and information, and participate in transparent, secure and well-governed digital services and commerce.

## 3. Mission

Design, integrate, curate and continuously improve accessible digital solutions and services. Build trust through transparent communication, responsible handling of data, clear commercial relationships and disciplined execution.

## 4. Vision

Become a recognised and trusted digital ecosystem with a coherent portfolio of useful products and services, sustainable business lines, strong technology foundations and a community that benefits from reliable information and support.

## 5. Values and operating behaviours

| Value | Required behaviour |
|---|---|
| Integrity | Do not overstate capabilities, returns, partnerships or results. |
| Transparency | Explain prices, terms, risk, sponsorship and affiliate relationships. |
| Customer value | Define the problem and intended outcome before building. |
| Privacy | Collect only necessary data and restrict access by role and purpose. |
| Security | Protect identities, accounts, information and operational systems. |
| Accountability | Give each activity an owner, approval route and measurable result. |
| Learning | Improve through feedback, experiments, tests and review. |
| Financial discipline | Track costs and contribution by business line; avoid unapproved commitments. |

## 6. Strategic pillars

### 6.1 Central In X Evolution governance

Purpose: coordinate brand strategy and portfolio direction.

Proposed responsibilities:
- Strategy, priorities and portfolio reviews.
- Brand and communications standards.
- Budget approval and cost allocation.
- Risk, security, privacy and compliance oversight.
- Shared supplier and partner governance.
- Consolidated performance reporting.

The word “Corporation” appears in a public-facing profile label, but that alone does not establish legal incorporation or a corporate group. Legal status must be verified separately.

### 6.2 In X Evolution Real Technology

Purpose: develop and deliver technology capabilities.

Offer hypotheses to validate:
- Websites and web applications.
- Custom software and workflow automation.
- API and third-party platform integrations.
- Data management, dashboards and reporting.
- Technical implementation, maintenance and support.
- Technology consulting and solution design.

A service should only be advertised after delivery capability, scope, support, security, pricing and contractual terms are established.

### 6.3 In X Evolution Invest

Purpose: provide a controlled area for investment-related information, research/education and internal investment or mining operations.

Offer hypotheses to validate:
- Educational material about digital financial tools and platforms.
- Research and comparison of third-party services.
- Internal records and monitoring for approved mining activities.
- Operational dashboards, activity logs and performance reporting.
- Disclosed referral relationships where permitted.

**Boundary:** do not assume that the brand may provide personal investment recommendations, execute trades for customers, hold customer assets, operate a collective investment scheme, provide payment services or custody crypto-assets. Those activities require activity-by-activity legal and regulatory analysis. Verify providers and permissions before promoting or integrating them.

An earlier user instruction identified Airtable as the intended enterprise operations platform for In X Evolution Invest and required preservation of three Mining records during implementation. The data model and integration boundaries must be approved; this repository does not prove that Airtable has been configured or that those records have been checked.

### 6.4 SHOP by In X Evolution

Purpose: provide a commercial discovery and transaction channel.

Offer hypotheses to validate:
- Curated product catalogue.
- Own-brand or third-party products.
- Digital products and downloadable resources.
- Referral or affiliate offers with clear disclosure.
- Order, fulfilment, support and returns workflows where In X Evolution is the seller.

The operating model must identify the seller of record, product owner, payment provider, fulfilment responsibility, refund policy and customer-support responsibility for every offer.

### 6.5 In X Evolution Real Telegram and community

Purpose: maintain direct communication and useful community channels.

Offer hypotheses to validate:
- Announcements and product updates.
- Educational content and tutorials.
- Community discussion and feedback.
- Customer support routing.
- Moderated specialist groups.

Community rules, advertising disclosures, moderation ownership, escalation and personal-data handling must be documented.

## 7. Strategic objectives

## 8. Business model hypotheses

These are options to test, not current revenue claims:

| Business line | Possible revenue model | Validation needed |
|---|---|---|
| Real Technology | Fixed-scope projects, implementation fees, support retainers, subscriptions | Customer demand, delivery cost, support load, margin |
| Invest | Educational products, research subscriptions or disclosed referral revenue where lawful | Regulatory review, audience need, provider terms, conflicts of interest |
| SHOP | Retail margin, digital product sales, disclosed affiliate commission | Seller status, conversion, fulfilment, returns, unit economics |
| Community | Paid membership, sponsorship or lead generation where appropriate | Community value, moderation cost, transparency and consent |
| Shared platform | Internal efficiencies; possible licensing only if a distinct product is validated | Reuse, differentiation, support obligations |

Do not count expected referrals, mining rewards, token values, unrealised gains or prospective sales as guaranteed revenue. Keep realised cash receipts separate from estimates and unrealised valuation.

## 9. Go-to-market approach

1. Select one priority business line rather than launching all lines simultaneously.
2. Define one user segment, a painful problem and a measurable outcome.
3. Interview representative users and review alternatives.
4. Formulate a minimum viable offer with explicit scope and price hypothesis.
5. Test demand through a small pilot or landing page.
6. Measure conversion, delivery cost, support effort and customer feedback.
7. Expand only when delivery and economics are repeatable.

## 10. Brand and portfolio rules

- Use one parent brand identity and clear sub-brand descriptors.
- Distinguish the project brand from any legally registered entity.
- Do not imply that third-party platforms are owned, endorsed or controlled by In X Evolution without an agreement.
- Label sponsored, affiliate and referral relationships.
- Keep product, customer and financial reporting separable by business line.
- Use shared services where this reduces duplication without creating inappropriate access to sensitive data.

## 11. Strategy approval conditions

The owner should approve:
- This purpose, mission and vision or provide a corrected version.
- Which public-facing portfolio names are active and owned.
- The first priority business line.
- The first target customer segment and validated problem.
- The first offer and its revenue hypothesis.
- The legal and regulatory boundaries for Invest and commerce.
- The resources and spending limit for the first pilot.
