# In X Evolution — Initial Enterprise Risk Register

**Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** Initial risk hypotheses; owner scoring required.

## Scoring
Assess Probability (P) and Impact (I) from 1 to 5. Score = P × I.

| Score | Level |
|---:|---|
| 1–5 | Low |
| 6–10 | Medium |
| 11–15 | High |
| 16–25 | Critical |

No numerical score is assigned until an owner assesses evidence.

## Required live-risk fields

Review high/critical risks before commitments and releases. Record risk acceptance, approver and review date.
