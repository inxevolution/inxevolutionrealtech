# In X Evolution — KPI Catalogue

**Version:** 0.1 · **Status:** Proposed metrics; numerical targets not set.

| KPI ID | Metric | Definition | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| KPI-001 | Qualified leads | Leads meeting approved qualification rules | Monthly | Business-line owner |
| KPI-002 | Conversion rate | Accepted customers/orders divided by qualified opportunities in a defined cohort | Monthly | Sales / line owner |
| KPI-003 | Revenue received | Cash received, separate from invoiced revenue | Monthly | Finance |
| KPI-004 | Gross margin | Revenue less direct costs under approved definitions | Monthly | Finance |
| KPI-005 | Contribution margin | Revenue less direct and approved variable costs | Monthly | Finance |
| KPI-006 | Customer retention | Customers retained in a defined period/cohort | Monthly/quarterly | Product owner |
| KPI-007 | Customer satisfaction | Results from a defined feedback method | Quarterly | Operations |
| KPI-008 | Delivery predictability | Planned versus completed work with scope changes visible | Per iteration | Delivery lead |
| KPI-009 | Defect escape rate | Defects found after release under a defined method | Per release | Technical lead |
| KPI-010 | Test reliability | Pass rate and stability of required automated tests | Per build | Technical lead |
| KPI-011 | Service availability | Measured uptime in an agreed window | Monthly | Operations |
| KPI-012 | Incident resolution time | Time from incident record to recovery or closure | Monthly | Operations |
| KPI-013 | Security remediation age | Age of open critical/high findings | Weekly/monthly | Security owner |
| KPI-014 | Budget variance | Actual plus committed spend versus approved budget | Monthly | Finance |
| KPI-015 | Data quality exceptions | Open duplicates, missing fields and reconciliation exceptions | Monthly | Data owner |

## Measurement rules
Every KPI needs an owner, source, formula, population, exclusions, time window and cadence. Define measurement before targets. Do not combine forecasts, token valuations or unpaid invoices with cash-received metrics.
