# Stakeholders and Roles

**Status:** Role model proposed; actual people and assignments are open.

## Stakeholders

- Founder / project owner.
- Executive or portfolio management.
- Business-line and product owners.
- Technical lead and delivery team.
- Finance and accounting adviser.
- Security and privacy lead.
- Legal and regulatory adviser.
- Operations and customer support.
- Marketing and community moderators.
- Customers and end users.
- Suppliers, technology providers and commercial partners.
- Regulators and public authorities where relevant.

## Proposed access roles

| Role | Access principle |
|---|---|
| Owner / super-admin | Approves strategy and privileged access; administrative access used sparingly |
| Portfolio manager | Consolidated operational view without unrestricted secrets or private data |
| Business-line manager | Access limited to their business line |
| Product owner | Product requirements, catalogue and acceptance evidence |
| Finance | Approved financial records and reconciliations |
| Technical administrator | Technical configuration and infrastructure |
| Sales / support | Assigned leads, customers and support cases |
| Marketing / community | Approved content and consented audience data |
| External partner | Specific, time-limited, least-privilege access |
| Auditor | Read-only access to authorised evidence |

A job title is not permission. Every access grant needs a business purpose, named owner, least-privilege scope, review date and revocation process.

Actual project owner, portfolio lead, product owner, technical lead, finance owner, privacy owner and support owner must be appointed.
