# Security and Privacy

**Status:** Proposed minimum control baseline.

## Core controls

- Individual identities for privileged access.
- Least privilege and periodic access review.
- MFA for privileged access where supported.
- Secrets held outside source control.
- Safe input validation, output encoding and query construction.
- Server-side authorisation for protected operations.
- Dependency and configuration review.
- Logs that avoid sensitive values.
- Environment separation.
- Tested backup and recovery.
- Incident escalation and post-incident review.

## Privacy

Before collecting personal data, document purpose, categories, data subjects, lawful basis, recipients/processors, transfers, retention, rights handling and safeguards. Assess GDPR applicability and national requirements for actual processing and legal roles.

## Invest safeguards

Do not promise financial returns. Verify external providers and commercial relationships. Do not custody customer assets, hold private keys, execute orders or provide regulated investment/payment services without activity-specific legal review and required permissions.

## SHOP safeguards

Identify seller of record, total price, tax, delivery, returns, warranty, payment provider and support responsibility. Disclose paid or affiliate relationships.
