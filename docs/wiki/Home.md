## Authoritative documents

The Wiki is a navigation and knowledge layer. Controlled baselines live in the repository's docs directory. Approved project documents and recorded decisions take precedence over informal Wiki explanations.

## Status labels

- **Confirmed:** verified repository fact or approved decision.
- **Proposed:** recommended approach awaiting approval.
- **Open:** requires evidence, owner decision or specialist review.
- **Implemented:** delivered and verified.
- **Not implemented:** documented intention only.

## Scope

This Wiki covers In X Evolution only. It does not imply that proposed business lines are separate legal entities, that services are live, or that regulated permissions exist.

## Contribution rules

Maintain one page per subject. Record owner, date, status and sources for material claims. Major policy and architecture changes must also update the relevant controlled document and, where appropriate, an ADR. Never add credentials, private keys, seed phrases, customer records or confidential operating data.
