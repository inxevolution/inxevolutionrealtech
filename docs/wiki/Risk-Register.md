## Assessment method

Probability (P) and Impact (I) use a scale from 1 to 5. Score = P × I.

| Score | Indicative level |
|---:|---|
| 1–5 | Low |
| 6–10 | Medium |
| 11–15 | High |
| 16–25 | Critical |

Do not assign numerical scores without evidence and an accountable owner.

A live risk record also needs cause, consequence, business line, response, due date, key risk indicator, residual risk and review date.
