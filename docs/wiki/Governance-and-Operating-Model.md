# Governance and Operating Model

**Status:** Proposed enterprise operating model.

## Governance layers

- **Strategic:** purpose, mission, vision, portfolio, risk appetite, priorities and budget.
- **Portfolio:** business-line business cases, performance, costs, risk and capacity.
- **Product:** product vision, backlog, requirements, acceptance and lifecycle.
- **Technical:** architecture, engineering standards, security, integration and technical debt.
- **Operational:** delivery, support, incidents, records, suppliers and continuity.

## Shared services and business-line autonomy

Shared capabilities may include brand standards, finance oversight, identity/access policies, procurement, legal coordination, data governance, reporting and security policy.

Each line remains accountable for its offers, customers, costs, workflows, support obligations and regulatory risks.

## Approval controls

Define approval thresholds for new products and public claims, spending, recurring commitments, suppliers, partner onboarding, data access, integrations, production releases, regulated activity changes and destructive data operations.

## Review cadence — proposed

| Review | Cadence | Output |
|---|---|---|
| Delivery coordination | Weekly during active work | Blockers, scope and release evidence |
| Business-line review | Monthly | KPIs, feedback, risk and costs |
| Portfolio review | Quarterly | Priorities, investment and resource allocation |
| Security/access review | At least quarterly or after material change | Access and risk actions |
| Strategy review | Annually or on material change | Objectives and portfolio fit |

## Segregation of duties

Avoid unchecked control of request, approval and reconciliation for high-risk operations. If team size prevents separation, record a compensating independent review.
