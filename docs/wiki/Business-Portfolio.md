# Business Portfolio

**Status:** Candidate structure; legal ownership and activity scope require confirmation.

| Area | Proposed role | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Central In X Evolution | Strategy, brand, governance, finance oversight and shared services | Does not itself establish a holding company or legal group |
| Real Technology | Software, websites, applications, automation, integrations and technical support | Advertise only capabilities that can be delivered and supported |
| Invest | Financial/digital-finance information, research and internal investment/mining operations | Regulated activities excluded pending specific legal review and authorisation |
| SHOP | Own products, third-party catalogue, digital commerce or affiliate offers | Seller of record, taxes, returns, fulfilment and disclosures must be clear |
| Real Telegram / Community | Communication, education, community and support routing | Moderation, consent, privacy and escalation must be defined |

## Portfolio governance

Each active business line must have an accountable owner, approved scope, target user, validated need, catalogue, budget, revenue model where applicable, risks, support process and review cadence.

## Expansion gate

A new line must not launch merely because it has a public name or social-media presence. Require an owner, business case, capacity assessment, legal review, cost envelope and measurable pilot.
