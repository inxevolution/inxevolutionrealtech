# Strategy, Mission and Vision

**Status:** Proposed; owner approval required.

## Purpose

Create and operate useful, trustworthy and commercially sustainable digital products and services that help people and organisations use technology, access relevant digital tools and information, and participate in well-governed digital communities and commerce.

## Mission

Design, integrate, curate and continuously improve accessible digital solutions and services. Operate transparently, protect user data, explain third-party relationships and build long-term value through responsible execution.

## Vision

Build a recognised, trusted and modular digital ecosystem in which technology, commerce, information and community reinforce one another without compromising user trust, security or regulatory responsibilities.

## Values

- Integrity and truthful communication.
- Innovation with practical customer value.
- Security and privacy.
- Transparency of prices, risks and commercial relationships.
- Accountability and documented ownership.
- Operational excellence and continuous improvement.
- Financial sustainability.

## Strategic objectives

| ID | Objective | Measurement candidates |
|---|---|---|
| STR-01 | Establish clear portfolio governance | Active lines with owner, scope, KPIs and risk register |
| STR-02 | Validate customer problems and offers | User evidence, pilot conversion and acceptance |
| STR-03 | Build reliable technology foundations | Test health, incidents, availability and delivery quality |
| STR-04 | Establish sustainable revenue | Revenue, gross margin and contribution by line |
| STR-05 | Build user trust | Retention, satisfaction, complaints and incident trends |
| STR-06 | Control financial and regulatory risk | Reviews completed, risks treated and reconciliations |
| STR-07 | Improve execution continuously | Delivery predictability and closed improvement actions |

Set numerical targets only after a baseline and budget are known. The owner must approve or amend the strategy before it becomes corporate policy.
