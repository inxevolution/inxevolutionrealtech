# Operations and Support

**Status:** Operating model proposed; no production service is currently documented.

## Operational ownership

Every live service must have a business owner, technical owner, support contact, severity model, monitoring, backup/recovery responsibility, vendor contacts and incident process.

## Incident lifecycle

Detect → classify → contain → investigate → recover → communicate → review and improve.

Record incident time, affected service, severity, impact, response actions, recovery evidence, owner and follow-up. Do not place secrets or unnecessary personal data in incident records.

## Service levels

Availability targets, support hours and response times must be agreed from user impact and operational capacity. Do not publish an SLA before the support model is funded and staffed.

## Backup and recovery

Define scope, frequency, encryption, retention, access, restore testing, RPO and RTO. A backup is not proven until restoration has been tested.

## Customer support

Define channels, request categories, ownership, response expectations, complaints, refunds and escalation. Keep community discussion separate from confidential support cases.

## Operational readiness

A service must not launch without assigned owners, documented support, monitoring, recovery and a tested release process.
