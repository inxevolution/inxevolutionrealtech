# 100-Year Longevity Plan

**Status:** Proposed stewardship framework · **Horizon:** 2026–2126.

## Core idea

Do not attempt to predict the next century. Build an organisation that can learn, replace technology, transfer responsibility, preserve knowledge and close responsibly if it no longer creates value.

## What should endure

- Purpose, integrity, transparency and accountability.
- Customer value and responsible innovation.
- Security, privacy and lawful operation.
- Financial discipline and stewardship.
- Traceable decisions and institutional memory.

## What must remain replaceable

Products, business models, revenue streams, suppliers, legal structures, data platforms, programming languages, frameworks and infrastructure.

## Six horizons

| Period | Primary focus |
|---|---|
| 0–1 year | Approve purpose, first pilot, owners and controls |
| 1–5 years | Prove customer value, supportability and sustainable economics |
| 5–15 years | Renew systems and suppliers; scale selectively |
| 15–30 years | Strengthen governance, archives and leadership succession |
| 30–50 years | Prepare generational transition and knowledge transfer |
| 50–100 years | Reassess relevance; continue, transform, transfer or close responsibly |

These are review horizons, not fixed delivery promises.

## Seven pillars

1. Purpose and governance continuity.
2. Financial durability and risk discipline.
3. Replaceable technology and architecture.
4. Long-term data and knowledge preservation.
5. Security and cryptographic agility.
6. People, culture and succession.
7. Legal, social and environmental adaptability.

## Non-negotiable safeguards

- No key person should be the only holder of critical knowledge or authority.
- No single vendor or proprietary format should make essential data unrecoverable.
- Backups must be restored and tested.
- Data retention must be lawful; not all records should be kept for 100 years.
- Cryptographic methods must be inventory-managed and replaceable.
- The organisation must be able to transfer or close a business line without losing unrelated records or obligations.
- No century-long survival or financial return is guaranteed.
