# Financial and Commercial Controls

**Status:** Control framework proposed; no approved budget or financial results are present in this repository.

## Budget discipline

Track approved budget, actual spend, committed spend and forecast by business line. Require an approver and purpose for recurring subscriptions, suppliers, contractors, marketing and infrastructure.

## Financial definitions

- Cash received is not the same as invoiced revenue.
- Gross margin deducts direct cost of delivery or goods.
- Contribution margin deducts direct and defined variable costs.
- Unrealised valuations are not cash.
- Mining rewards, token prices and referrals are not guaranteed revenue.
- Refunds, chargebacks and fees must be visible.

## Commercial approval

Before publishing an offer, approve scope, price, taxes, seller/provider, delivery, contract, refund/returns, warranty, support and disclosure requirements.

## Invest boundary

Do not hold customer funds or private keys under this baseline. Verify provider terms, authorisations, jurisdiction, custody and withdrawal conditions. Seek legal/accounting review for potentially regulated activities.

Airtable may support operations but must not be treated as statutory accounting, bank, payment or regulated custody infrastructure.
