# Products and Services

**Status:** Candidate offers; not an approved live catalogue.

## Real Technology

Candidate services: website and application development, custom software, automation, API integration, dashboards, technical consulting, maintenance and support.

Approval evidence: demonstrated capability, scope, delivery method, acceptance criteria, pricing, support, security and contracts.

## Invest

Candidate services: factual digital-finance education, sourced platform research, internal operations registers, mining activity monitoring and reporting.

General information is not automatically personalised investment advice. Do not assume permission for custody, execution, portfolio management, payment services or other regulated activities. Obtain activity-specific legal and regulatory review.

Airtable is the intended operational platform for the Invest workstream, subject to approval. Preserve the three Mining records identified by the project owner before migrations or destructive operations.

## SHOP

Candidate offers: own-brand goods, third-party products, digital products and disclosed affiliate listings.

For every offer, identify the seller of record, product owner, full price, tax, delivery, returns, warranty, payment provider and support responsibility.

## Community / Real Telegram

Candidate services: official announcements, educational content, moderated discussion, product support routing and feedback.

Define community rules, moderation, escalation, privacy and advertising disclosure.

## Product launch checklist

- [ ] Product owner and target user identified.
- [ ] Problem and demand validated.
- [ ] Description, scope and limitations approved.
- [ ] Price, costs and commercial model reviewed.
- [ ] Legal, privacy and security reviews completed.
- [ ] Provider and partner relationships verified.
- [ ] Support, complaints, refunds and incident ownership defined.
- [ ] Acceptance criteria and pilot metrics set.
- [ ] Launch approval recorded.
