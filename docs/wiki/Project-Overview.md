# Project Overview

**Status:** Proposed strategic definition.

In X Evolution is proposed as a brand-led digital business ecosystem that creates, integrates, curates and operates useful digital products and services across technology, digital commerce, investment-related information and online communities.

This definition does not establish a registered corporate group, a particular legal entity, live products or permission to provide regulated financial services.

## Purpose

Create useful, trustworthy and commercially sustainable digital products and services that help people and organisations use technology, access relevant digital tools and information, and participate in transparent digital services and commerce.

## Current stage

Strategy and discovery. The repository contains project documentation but no application source code, deployment configuration or production software.

## Candidate portfolio

- Central governance and shared enterprise operations.
- In X Evolution Real Technology.
- In X Evolution Invest.
- SHOP by In X Evolution.
- In X Evolution Real Telegram / community.

Each area needs an owner, scope, operating model, budget and risk assessment before launch.

## Immediate priorities

1. Approve purpose, mission, vision and legal/ownership structure.
2. Select one initial business line.
3. Validate target users and their problem.
4. Approve the first offer and acceptance criteria.
5. Confirm legal, financial and technical constraints.
6. Approve a costed pilot.
