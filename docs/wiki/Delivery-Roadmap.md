# Delivery Roadmap

**Status:** Proposed sequence; no delivery dates or budgets approved.

1. **Repository foundation:** project charter, strategy, requirements, architecture, security, finance and governance documentation.
2. **Discovery:** legal structure, target users, first business line, validated problem, first offer, budget and regulatory boundaries.
3. **Solution design:** workflows, data ownership, C4 views, threat model, stack, costed hosting and provider integrations.
4. **Technical foundation:** reproducible environments, CI, identity/access, data/API boundaries, tests, secrets and monitoring.
5. **Pilot:** one approved capability, E2E tests, user feedback, delivery-cost and operational measurements.
6. **Production readiness:** security/privacy/legal review, backups, incident response, support, monitoring and release approval.
7. **Improvement and expansion:** use customer outcomes, reliability and unit economics to decide next investment.

A phase is complete only when deliverables, owner, risks, costs and exit evidence are recorded. Dates and numerical targets require an approved plan.
