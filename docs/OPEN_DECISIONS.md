# In X Evolution — Open Decisions Register

**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 23 November 2026

This register prevents assumptions from silently becoming business or technical commitments. Every decision needs an owner, evidence, impact assessment and approval. Items are prioritised for Phase 1.

## Decision record template

Each material decision should have:
1. ID, title, date, status and approver.
2. Context and evidence.
3. Options considered.
4. Decision and rationale.
5. Financial, legal, security and operational consequences.
6. Dependencies and reversibility.
7. Review date.

## Closure rule

An item is closed only when the answer, supporting evidence, owner and approval are recorded. A public brand label, technology preference or marketing link is not sufficient evidence by itself.
