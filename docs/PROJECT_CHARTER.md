# In X Evolution — Project Charter

**Version:** 0.2  
**Date:** 23 November 2026  
**Status:** Strategic baseline proposed for owner approval

## 1. Purpose

In X Evolution is proposed as a brand-led digital business ecosystem that develops, operates and coordinates a portfolio of technology, digital commerce, investment-related information and community activities.

Its purpose is to create useful, trustworthy and commercially sustainable digital products and services that help people and organisations use technology, access relevant digital tools and information, and participate in well-governed digital communities and commerce.

This definition describes the intended project and brand direction. It does not establish that all proposed services exist, that the brand is a registered corporate group, or that the project is authorised to provide regulated financial services.

## 2. Mission

Design, integrate, curate and continuously improve accessible digital solutions and services. Operate them transparently, protect user data, explain third-party relationships and build long-term value through responsible execution.

## 3. Vision

Build a recognised, trusted and modular digital ecosystem in which technology, commerce, information and community reinforce one another without compromising user trust, security or regulatory responsibilities.

## 4. Values

- **Integrity:** accurate claims, clear ownership and honest communication.
- **Transparency:** visible terms, prices, risks, sponsorships and affiliate relationships.
- **User trust:** protect user accounts, information and choices.
- **Security and privacy:** minimise data, restrict access and design safeguards early.
- **Utility:** solve identifiable user problems rather than adding features without purpose.
- **Continuous improvement:** use feedback, metrics, tests and evidence.
- **Accountability:** assign owners and record material decisions.
- **Sustainability:** control commitments and pursue positive unit economics.

## 6. Proposed portfolio boundaries

| Portfolio area | Proposed responsibility | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Central In X Evolution governance | Strategy, portfolio, finance, brand, risk and shared operations | Does not automatically imply a registered holding company or legal group. |
| In X Evolution Real Technology | Software, digital products, automation, integration and technical services | Specific offers, skills, service levels and liability terms require approval. |
| In X Evolution Invest | Investment-related information, research/education and internal investment/mining operations | No custody, execution, portfolio management or personalised investment advice unless legal review confirms the required authorisation and operating model. |
| SHOP by In X Evolution | Product catalogue, commerce and/or curated partner offers | Must distinguish own-sold goods from third-party and affiliate offers. |
| In X Evolution Real Telegram | Community, communication, learning and support | Community moderation, privacy and support responsibilities must be defined. |

The public-facing [In X Evolution Invest profile](https://linktr.ee/gabrielinvestment) appears to list these brand labels and third-party digital finance, exchange, mining and earning platforms. Treat this as a research lead, not proof of the legal entity, ownership, formal product catalogue or regulatory permissions.

## 9. Scope

### Included

- Strategy, governance and portfolio definition for In X Evolution.
- Requirements, technical architecture and decision records.
- Approved technology and commercial products/services.
- Shared customer, partner, financial-control and operational processes.
- Security, privacy, testing, release and support practices.

### Excluded unless explicitly approved

- Unrelated initiatives and projects.
- Unverified corporate entities or business claims.
- Handling customer funds, private keys or financial assets.
- Providing regulated investment, payment, credit or crypto-asset services without the relevant legal assessment and authorisation.
- Production integrations, infrastructure commitments or external account changes not explicitly approved.

## 10. Enterprise operating model — proposed

Use a portfolio model with shared governance and controlled separation of business lines:

- Shared: identity, brand standards, governance, risk, procurement, finance oversight, data policies and reporting.
- Business-line specific: product ownership, offer catalogue, customer workflows, operating procedures, cost/revenue attribution and KPIs.
- Restricted: financial records, personal data, security administration and sensitive business information, accessible only to authorised roles.

Shared systems must preserve business-line separation in permissions, data ownership and reporting.

## 12. Initial success criteria

- The owner approves the purpose, mission, vision and initial strategic priorities.
- At least one priority customer segment and problem are validated.
- The first product/service offer has a defined scope, price hypothesis and acceptance criteria.

- A costed and affordable first delivery increment is approved.
- All active business lines have accountable owners and measurable outcomes.
